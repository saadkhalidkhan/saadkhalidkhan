## About me

I'm a **Senior Mobile Engineer** with **8+ years** of experience in Android development and the Java ecosystem. I work as a **Senior Android Engineer** at **Systems Limited**, Lahore, building production apps for **banking and enterprise** with **Kotlin**, **Jetpack Compose**, **Firebase**, **Room**, **Retrofit**, and **Coroutines / Flow**.

I design and ship scalable Android solutions using **SOLID**, **Clean Architecture**, **MVVM**, and **modular** Gradle setups — across banking, diagnostics, education, ERP, and research platforms. I collaborate with cross-functional and distributed teams in **Agile/Scrum** environments.

Outside client work, I publish **Jetpack Compose libraries** on [Maven Central](https://central.sonatype.com/search?q=saadkhalidkhan) and share engineering notes on [Medium](https://medium.com/@saadkhan0799).
